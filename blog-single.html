GLP-1 Science

Understanding GLP-1 and Metabolic Set Points

Dr. Elena Rostova

Oct 12, 2026 • 5 min read

For decades, the prevailing advice for weight loss was a simple equation: eat less, move more. However, modern endocrinology has shown us that metabolic pathways are far more complex.

The Biological Set Point

Your body has a preferred weight, often referred to as a "set point." When you try to lose weight through sheer calorie restriction, your body fights back by increasing hunger hormones (ghrelin) and decreasing satiety signals.

"We are not fighting a lack of willpower; we are fighting evolutionary biology."

How GLP-1 Changes the Game

Glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonists work by mimicking a hormone naturally produced in your intestines. They slow gastric emptying and signal the brain that you are full, effectively quieting "food noise."
